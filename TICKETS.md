## SUP-2291: Charged a late fee for a book I returned on time

**From:** Sam Altman (patron, San Francisco Library)
**Card:** 21000101

> I returned The Salt Road on the day it was due, I checked the receipt before I went. I still got a 25 cent late fee. This is the second time this has happened to me. I always come in after work. My neighbor goes in the mornings and says she has never had this problem. It's only a quarter but it's the principle. Can you take it off and make sure it doesn't keep happening?

**Note from the San Francisco desk:** We get a few of these a week. We waive them when people ask, but most people probably don't ask.

## SUP-2304: My hold was "ready" but the book wasn't there

**From:** Zohran Mamdani (patron, New York City Library)
**Card:** 21000202

> I got the email saying The Lighthouse Keeper's Daughter was ready for pickup. I took the subway 30 minutes to the library the next morning and it wasn't on the hold shelf. The app still says it's waiting for me. The person at the desk looked it up and said someone else checked it out the same afternoon it was returned. I've been on the waiting list for six weeks. How does this happen?

**Note from the New York City desk:** This happens a few times a month, almost always on busy afternoons when the return bin is full and the self-checkout line is long. Some staff have started leaving returns on a cart for an hour before shelving them, which seems to help, but we don't know why.

## SUP-2318: App says copies are available but the shelf is empty

**From:** Several patrons, mostly at San Francisco Library

> The app said there were 2 copies of Pacific Tides Field Guide at the San Francisco library. I went over and there were none. The librarian said they were both on the hold shelf for other people.

**Note from the San Francisco desk:** We hear this a lot, mostly about things people placed holds on recently. Closing the app and searching again does not fix it. It usually sorts itself out by the next day.
